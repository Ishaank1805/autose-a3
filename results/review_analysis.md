For each review of a **defective** seeded PR, every probe script the reviewer wrote and ran was re-run on the seeded PR and on the correct reference fix of the same issue. A probe *exposes* the defect when its result differs between the two. "Approved anyway" means the reviewer ran a probe whose output showed the defect and still approved; "probes never exposed it" means its probes did not touch the defect.
